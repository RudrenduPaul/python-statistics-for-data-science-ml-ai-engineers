## Addendum: visual QA and storytelling-gap pass

Rechecked every figure's underlying math against a live browser render, not just against the
code. Found and fixed two bugs:

1. `fig-khat-diagnostic`: the two "planted outlier" points never produced a k-hat anywhere near
   the 0.7 warning line the caption claimed they would, regardless of how extreme the y-value
   contamination was made (tested shifts up to 500ms, sample sizes down to n=5, and an unknown-
   variance model). Root cause, confirmed analytically and numerically: in a closed-form
   conjugate-Gaussian fit, the negative log-likelihood of a single point across posterior draws
   is a quadratic function of a Gaussian variable, which has a provably light (chi-squared-type)
   tail no matter the data. Fixed by giving the two outlier points a small-probability, power-
   law-distributed chance per posterior draw of being badly missed by the model, the standard
   mechanism for heavy-tailed importance ratios, documented in the code with the reasoning.
   Caption updated to describe the resulting two-tier pattern (one point past 0.7, one in the
   0.5-0.7 watch zone) instead of a vaguer claim.
2. `fig-waic-ploo-complexity`: with a single noise feature, the training-fit gain (~0.3 points)
   and the WAIC/PSIS-LOO penalty (~0.4 points) were present but invisible on a chart spanning
   ~330 units. Fixed by using fifteen noise features instead of one, which produces a training
   gain and a WAIC/PSIS-LOO penalty large enough to see. Chapter text updated to match, and now
   explicitly explains why fifteen features were used (visibility) while pointing to the
   later single-noise-feature table for the small-effect-size case.

No storytelling gaps found needing a new figure: every major concept (lpd, WAIC, PSIS-LOO,
k-hat, model comparison) has a figure, and the chapter's "when this is worth the setup cost"
